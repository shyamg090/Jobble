# Jobble - Tinder-Style Job Marketplace

A modern job marketplace that connects **Job Seekers** with **Recruiters** through a swipe-based interface.

## 🎯 Overview

Jobble reimagines job hunting as a matching experience:
- **Job Seekers** swipe through job postings and express interest
- **Recruiters** swipe through candidates and send connection requests
- Matches lead to chat conversations and potential hires
